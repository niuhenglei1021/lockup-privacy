# 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

**锁上（LockUp）**
生效日期：2026年11月2日

锁上是装在你自己 iPhone 上的应用，用来按你的设置锁定或隐藏应用。我们没有自己的账号服务器，也不把你的密码、应用名单或使用记录上传给我们。

## 我们不收集的内容

我们不收集姓名、电子邮箱、电话号码、通讯录、定位、广告标识，也不使用第三方统计或广告工具。应用里没有注册账号。

## 只保存在这台手机上的数据

- **应用密码。** 你设置的 4 到 8 位数字密码保存在这台 iPhone 的钥匙串里，用来打开锁上、提前结束锁定或恢复隐藏。我们看不到这组密码。删除应用后，钥匙串里的密码可能仍留在这台手机上，直到你抹掉整台设备。
- **锁定和隐藏设置。** 你选择的应用、时长、每天自动锁定的时间段，保存在这台手机的应用组数据里，用来在时间到了之后继续执行或结束。这些内容不会发给我们。
- **本地通知。** 锁定或隐藏结束时，系统可能在这台手机上显示一条本地通知。通知由系统在本机发出，不经过我们的服务器。

## 屏幕使用时间

锁定和隐藏使用苹果的屏幕使用时间（Family Controls）。你要在系统授权后，应用才能限制你选中的应用。相关权限由 iOS 管理。我们不会把你选择的应用名单传到自己的服务器。你可以随时在「设置 → 屏幕使用时间」里关闭这项权限。

## 订阅

月度会员和年度会员由苹果的 App Store 收款、续订和退款。付款信息由苹果处理，我们收不到你的银行卡号或 Apple ID 密码。应用只在这台手机上向 App Store 查询当前订阅是否有效。试用、价格和取消方式以购买时的 App Store 页面为准。你可以在「设置 → Apple ID → 订阅」里取消。

## 儿童

锁上供设备主人自己使用，不以 13 岁以下儿童为对象。我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

## 政策变更

如果这份说明有实质变更，我们会更新本页的生效日期。继续使用锁上，即表示你了解更新后的内容。

# Privacy Policy

**LockUp**
Effective date: November 2, 2026

LockUp runs on your iPhone and locks or hides apps according to your settings. We do not operate an account server, and we do not upload your password, app list, or usage history to ourselves.

## What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, or advertising identifier. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising. The app has no account registration.

## Data that stays on this iPhone

- **App password.** The 4 to 8 digit password you set is stored in this iPhone’s Keychain. It is used to open LockUp, end a lock early, or restore hidden apps. We cannot see it. After you delete the app, the Keychain item may remain on this iPhone until you erase the device.
- **Lock and hide settings.** The apps, durations, and daily lock window you choose are stored in this iPhone’s app group so a session can continue or end on time. They are not sent to us.
- **Local notifications.** When a lock or hide ends, iOS may show a local notification on this iPhone. It is created on the device and does not pass through our server.

## Screen Time

Locking and hiding use Apple’s Screen Time (Family Controls). The app can limit the apps you select only after you grant that permission. iOS manages the permission. We do not transmit your selected apps to our own server. You can turn the permission off in Settings → Screen Time.

## Subscriptions

Monthly and yearly memberships are billed, renewed, and refunded by Apple’s App Store. Apple processes payment. We do not receive your card number or Apple ID password. The app only asks the App Store, on this iPhone, whether the current subscription is active. The trial, price, and cancellation terms shown at purchase are the ones that apply. You can cancel in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

## Children

LockUp is for the owner of the device. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

If this notice changes in a material way, we will update the effective date on this page. Continuing to use LockUp means you have had a chance to read the updated notice.
